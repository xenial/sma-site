Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

This policy explains what SMA stores about editors and labels who use the SMA Discord server and the SMA bot, why we store it, and how to delete it.

Who we are

SMA runs the SMA Discord server and the SMA bot, and is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: hello@xeni.al.

What we collect from editors

  • Discord: your user ID, the roles the bot gives you, and your application answers: TikTok handle, niche, editing tools, portfolio links and country.
  • TikTok, when you sign in with TikTok: the data in the table below.
  • TikTok sign-in tokens, stored encrypted, so the bot can refresh your stats without asking you to sign in again.
  • Viewer-country screenshots you upload, and the countries and percentages read from them.
  • Your scores, tier, country factor and strikes, and a log of decisions about your account.
  • Campaign videos you submit: their links and view counts.
  • Payout details, once payouts start: your PayPal email or crypto wallet address, and the identity and tax details needed to pay you.

TikTok data

SMA uses TikTok Login Kit. It only reads data and never posts, comments or sends messages for you.

PermissionWhat SMA readsWhy
user.info.basicAccount ID, display name, profile pictureTo confirm the account is yours and is linked to only one Discord account
user.info.profileUsername, profile link, verified badgeTo show staff which account applied
user.info.statsFollower, following, like and video countsTo score your account and set your tier
video.listPublic videos from the last 90 days: views, likes, comments, shares, post date, linkTo score your recent videos, check that campaign videos came from your account, and count their views for pay

SMA does not access your messages, drafts or private videos. You can disconnect SMA at any time in the TikTok app's settings under app permissions.

What we collect from labels

Label name, contact person, billing email, campaign details and payment records.

This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other sites. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitor IP addresses for security, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Why we use it

  • To run the service you signed up for: deciding applications, setting tiers and rates, running campaigns and paying you.
  • To prevent fraud and keep SMA working, such as spotting bought views and fake screenshots. This is our legitimate interest.
  • To keep accounting and tax records that the law requires.

Who sees it

  • SMA staff see applications, stats, screenshots and decisions.
  • Labels see the videos submitted to their campaigns and those videos' stats.
  • Service providers process data for us:
    • Discord, where the server and bot run
    • our hosting provider, which runs the bot and its database
    • Anthropic, which reads viewer-country screenshots when automatic reading is turned on
    • PayPal and crypto networks for payouts. Crypto transactions are public on their blockchain.

Some of these providers process data outside the EU and EEA under their own data protection terms. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

We keep your data until you delete it or ask us to. Payment records are kept as long as accounting law requires, even after you leave.

Your rights

  • Delete your data: run /delete-my-data in Discord. It removes your application, TikTok link, stats and screenshots, revokes SMA's TikTok access and removes your SMA roles.
  • Other requests: email hello@xeni.al to ask us to show, correct or export your data, or to object to how we use it.
  • Complaints: you can complain to the data protection authority where you live. In Finland, that is the Data Protection Ombudsman.

Young users

SMA has no age limit of its own, but you must meet TikTok's and Discord's minimum ages. Where the law requires it, a parent or guardian must agree for younger users.

Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and announce it in the SMA Discord.

Contact

Email hello@xeni.al, or message SMA staff in the SMA Discord server.

SMA is independent and not affiliated with TikTok or Discord.
