Connect your TikTok to SMA

Signing in with TikTok lets the SMA bot read your public stats to set your tier and pay rate. SMA asks TikTok for these permissions:

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): your account ID, display name and profile picture
  • Profile details (user.info.profile): your username, profile link and verified badge
  • Stats (user.info.stats): your follower, following, like and video counts
  • Public videos (video.list): views, likes, comments, shares and dates of your public videos

SMA only reads data. It never posts, comments or sends messages for you.
