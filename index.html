How it works

Everything runs inside the SMA Discord server, through the SMA bot.

For editors and clippers

  1. Apply in the Discord and sign in with TikTok. The bot reads your public stats and sets your tier.
  2. Upload a screenshot of where your viewers are. It sets your country factor.
  3. Join a campaign in #campaigns to get the sound and the brief.
  4. Post your edit with the sound, then submit the link to the bot.
  5. Get paid weekly by PayPal or crypto for the views your edit earns.

For labels and artists

  1. Get verified and receive a private channel in the server.
  2. Create a campaign: one TikTok sound, a budget, and a rate per video or per 1,000 views.
  3. Fund the budget. SMA adds a 7% platform fee on top.
  4. Vetted editors post with your sound. Follow views and spend in your channel every day.

Pay that follows your numbers

Your rate = the campaign's base rate × your tier multiplier × your country factor

Your tier comes from your recent views, engagement, posting rhythm and followers. Your country factor runs from 0.25 to 1.0, depending on where your viewers live. Both are re-checked regularly, so they move with your account.

Example: on a campaign paying $1.00 per 1,000 views, a Pro editor whose viewers are mostly in the US earns about $0.92 per 1,000 views.

How SMA uses TikTok

SMA uses TikTok Login Kit so editors can sign in with TikTok and share their public stats with the SMA bot. SMA only reads data. It never posts, comments or sends messages for you.

PermissionWhat SMA readsWhy
user.info.basic Your TikTok account ID, display name and profile picture To confirm the account is yours and is linked to only one Discord account
user.info.profile Your username, profile link and verified badge To show SMA staff which account applied and to link to it
user.info.stats Your follower, following, like and video counts To score your account and set your tier
video.list Your public videos: views, likes, comments, shares, post date and link To score your recent videos, check that campaign videos came from your account, and count their views for pay

SMA never:

  • posts, edits or deletes anything on your TikTok account
  • reads your messages, drafts or private videos
  • sells your data

TikTok sign-in does not share where your viewers live, so you upload a screenshot of TikTok Analytics for that instead. You can disconnect SMA at any time in TikTok's app permissions, or delete everything with /delete-my-data in Discord. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
